How to use this

Do the day you are on, check the boxes as you go, and stop when you are done. Each day is about 60–120 minutes. If you miss a day, do not double up — drop the lowest-value task and keep moving. If your exam is before November 16, 2026, spend extra time on the Deploy days; on or after that date, lean into Plan and Design.

The rule that matters most

Review every practice exam the day after you take it, and read the explanation for every question, including the ones you got right. Architect questions are about why the other three options are worse; that reasoning is what transfers to the real exam.
