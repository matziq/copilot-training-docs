Scaled score estimator

Enter how many you got right out of how many you answered, and this converts it to an estimate on the 1–1,000 scale Microsoft reports. Useful for scoring any practice material, including the official practice assessment.

What this number is and is not

Microsoft does not publish its raw-to-scaled conversion, and questions can carry different weights. This curve puts 70% correct at 700. Treat it as a readiness signal with a margin of error of roughly ±50 points, not a prediction.

Error log

Every question you miss goes in here — not the question, the rule. “Use environment variables, not hard-coded URLs, so the solution moves cleanly between environments” is a log entry. “Missed question 14” is not.

Tag the cause, because each cause has a different fix

Never knew it means learn the fact — guides and flashcards. Knew it and read past it means slow down and list the constraints before reading options. Misread the question means you missed a least effort, a must, or a NOT — a reading habit, and the cheapest one to fix.
