Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect

AB-100 tests whether you can plan, design, and deploy agent-based business solutions across Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, Microsoft Foundry, Dynamics 365, and Power Platform. It is an architect exam: most questions describe a business with constraints and ask for the best design choice, not a configuration click path.

Strategy in one line

If your date is before November 16, 2026, Deploy (monitoring, testing, ALM, governance) is nearly half the exam. On or after that date, Plan and Design together are about 70%. Either way, the exam rewards picking the least complex Microsoft option that meets every stated requirement.

Exam-day checklist

Before

  • Run the system test the day before if you test online, and clear your desk.
  • Bring a valid government ID that matches your Microsoft certification profile name.
  • Skim the three cheat sheets at the bottom of each guide — nothing new.
  • Sleep. A rested architect reads constraints; a tired one reads keywords.

During

  • Read the requirement list first, then the question. Underline residency, cost, latency, and “least effort” clues.
  • Case studies can lock once you leave them. Finish each case before moving on.
  • Watch for “Each correct answer presents part of the solution” — multiple-select items.
  • Never leave anything blank. There is no penalty for a wrong answer.
