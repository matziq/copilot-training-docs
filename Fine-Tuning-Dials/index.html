Fine-Tuning Dials

An observatory control panel for the constants of nature.

Outcome bands are grounded in the physics literature on cosmic fine-tuning (Rees, Just Six Numbers; Barrow & Tipler, The Anthropic Cosmological Principle; the Hoyle carbon-12 resonance; Penrose's entropy argument) but this is an educational simulation, not a physics engine — band boundaries are illustrative approximations, not precision calculations.
