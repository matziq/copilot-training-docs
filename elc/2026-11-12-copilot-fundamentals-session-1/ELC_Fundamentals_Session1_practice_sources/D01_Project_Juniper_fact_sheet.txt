Holiday gift set sampling pilot
Prepared by: Priya Natarajan, Program Lead, Consumer Engagement
Prepared for: Jordan Avery, Project Coordinator (joined the team on Wednesday, November 11, 2026)
Status: Approved for pilot on Monday, November 9, 2026
Purpose: Test whether in-store sampling of the Corvelle Winter Ritual gift set increases gift set sales.
Pilot dates: Friday, November 27, 2026 through Thursday, December 24, 2026
Pilot stores: 12 partner stores - Northeast 5, Midwest 4, West 3
Sample kits: 18,000 deluxe sample kits (1,500 per store)
Budget: USD 96,000 total
- Sample kits: USD 54,000
- Brand ambassador staffing: USD 30,000

Success measures:
- Gift set sell-through at least 15% higher than in matched non-pilot stores
- At least 2,000 new loyalty sign-ups across the pilot stores
- Average customer survey score of 4.3 or higher out of 5
