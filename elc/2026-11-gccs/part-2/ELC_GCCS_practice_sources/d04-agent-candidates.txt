Answers recurring questions about approved protocols, templates, and study-status files in one governed SharePoint library. Must cite files and respect permissions. No actions. Proposed owner: Taylor Brooks.

Reads draft study results and automatically approves consumer-facing product claims. Would require scientific, legal, regulatory, and brand authority. No owner identified.
C. Weekly Evidence Briefing Assistant
Creates a draft weekly summary from a defined SharePoint folder. Separates new documents, changed decisions, risks, and missing evidence. Human reviews before distribution. Proposed owner: Jordan Lee.
D. One-Time Meeting Recap
Summarizes a single supplied transcript for one project checkpoint. No ongoing owner needed.
E. Participant Data Explorer
Answers unrestricted questions across row-level participant records, including sensitive data, without a defined access model or approved purpose. No owner identified.
Scoring criteria (0 = no, 1 = partly, 2 = yes):
1. Repeats often
2. Has a stable audience and job
3. Has a bounded, maintained knowledge source
4. Can cite evidence
5. Has clear human review
6. Avoids authority it should not have
7. Has an owner who can test and maintain it
