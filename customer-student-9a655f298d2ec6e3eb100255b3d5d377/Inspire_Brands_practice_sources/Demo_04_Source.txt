Calculation: VarianceUnits = ActualUnits minus PlannedUnits.
Calculation: VariancePct = VarianceUnits divided by PlannedUnits.
Flag rule: Review if the absolute VariancePct is greater than or equal to 10%; otherwise OK.
Zero-plan rule: if PlannedUnits is zero, show VariancePct as Not calculable and Flag as Review; do not divide by zero or use a fabricated percentage.
Total rule: calculate total VariancePct from total actual and total planned, not by averaging site percentages.
Draft manager claim to verify: "No site crossed the review threshold." Treat this as an unverified claim, not a data row.
