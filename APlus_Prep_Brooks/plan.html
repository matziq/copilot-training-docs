How to use this

Do the day you are on, check the boxes as you go, and stop when you are done. Checkmarks save automatically in this browser, and each exam keeps its own separate list. If you miss a day, do not double up the next day — drop the lowest-value task instead and keep moving. Momentum beats volume.

The one rule that matters most

Never review a practice exam on the same day you take it. Your brain is still defending its answers. Sleep on it, come back the next morning, and you will see mistakes that were invisible the night before. Every exam day in this plan is followed by a repair day for exactly that reason.
