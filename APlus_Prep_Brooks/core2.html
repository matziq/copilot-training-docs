How Core 2 differs from Core 1

A higher bar and a flatter shape

Core 1 passes at 675. Core 2 passes at 700 — roughly 75% correct instead of 72%. On a 90-question exam that is about three extra right answers, which does not sound like much until you are sitting on 68.

Core 1 is lopsided: two domains are over half the exam, so you can triage. Core 2 is evenly weighted — 28, 28, 23, 21 — which means there is no domain small enough to write off.

The domain people throw away

Operational Procedures is 21% of Core 2 and gets skipped constantly because it is “not technical.” It is backup types, safety rules, change management, ticket fields, chain of custody, and how to talk to an angry customer.

There is no hardware knowledge in it at all, which makes it the cheapest 21% on the entire A+. Skipping it is the single most common avoidable way to fail this exam.

How the plan works

Fourteen days ending the day before your exam.

1

Two weeks, not nine days

Core 2 gets a longer plan than Core 1 because its four domains do not overlap with each other the way Core 1’s do. There is simply more distinct material to cover.

2

Three exams, three repairs

A cold diagnostic on day 1, then two full-length exams with a repair day after each. The repair day is where the score actually moves.

3

Order matters here

Core 2 tests sequences literally: the seven malware-removal steps and the change-management order. Memorize them as lists, in order, out loud.

The three things Core 2 asks over and over

What comes next? — the malware removal order and the troubleshooting methodology. Which tool? — given a Windows symptom, name the one utility that solves it. Which is more restrictive? — NTFS versus share permissions combining over a network. Get those three reflexes solid and you have covered a surprising share of the exam.

Exam day logistics

The night before

  • Confirm the appointment and whether it is in-center or online proctored — the rules are completely different.
  • Two forms of ID, at least one government-issued with a photo and signature. Names must match the booking exactly.
  • If it is online proctored: run the system test, clear the desk down to bare wood, and check the walls.
  • Check the exam code on the confirmation reads 220-1102.
  • Stop studying by 8:00 pm. Lights out early.

The morning of

  • Eat protein, not just sugar. Do not skip breakfast.
  • Arrive thirty minutes early. Late arrivals get turned away and lose the fee.
  • Warm up by reciting the seven malware-removal steps and the backup types. Do not learn anything new.
  • Do the performance-based questions last. Flag them, clear the multiple choice, then come back.
  • Read the last line first. BEST, FIRST, and NEXT change the answer.
  • Answer every single question. There is no penalty for being wrong.
