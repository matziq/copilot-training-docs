Enter how many you got right out of how many you answered, and this converts it to the 100–900 scale the way CompTIA reports it. Useful for scoring any practice material, not just the exams on this site.

What this number is and is not

CompTIA has never published its raw-to-scaled conversion, and the real exam weights harder questions more heavily. This curve is anchored so that 72% correct lands on 675, which is where the passing mark is generally understood to sit. Treat it as a readiness signal with a margin of error of roughly ±40 points, not as a prediction.

Error log

The single highest-value habit in the whole plan. Every question you miss goes in here — not the question, the rule. “RAID 5 needs three drives, not two” is a log entry. “Missed question 14” is not.

Tag the cause, because the three causes have three different fixes

Never knew it means learn the fact — flashcards. Knew it and read past it means slow down and read the last line first — that is a pacing and care problem, and more studying will not touch it. Misread the question means you missed a BEST, a FIRST, or a NOT — that is a reading habit, and it is the cheapest one to fix.
