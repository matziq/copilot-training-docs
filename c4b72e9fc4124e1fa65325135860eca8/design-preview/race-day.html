ALL RACE INFORMATION ↗
What does registration cost?

Early registration is $20 before November 2; standard online registration is $25 beginning November 2. Race-day registration is $30 from 8–9 AM. Review final charges on RaceEntry.

Is this a timed race?

Race timing and results are included. Race-day shirts and timing chips are first come, first served. Check registration details for current availability.

Can I walk the whole course?

Yes. Walkers are welcome and should line up toward the back of the field so faster participants can move safely.

What about kids, strollers, and dogs?

Children are welcome and need individual registrations. Stroller and dog policies are not confirmed in the current 2026 information; contact the organizers before race day.

November 26. West Minico. We'll see you there.
