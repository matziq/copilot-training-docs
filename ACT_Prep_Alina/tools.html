The arithmetic worth memorizing

Because the composite averages three sections, every 3 points you add across English, Math, and Reading is worth 1 composite point. Three points is easiest to find in your weakest section, which is why Math gets most of your time.

Error log

This is the most valuable tool on the site and the one students skip. After every test and every drill, write down what you missed and why. Sorting misses by cause tells you whether your problem is knowledge, speed, or attention — and those three have completely different fixes.

The three causes
  • Never knew it — a real content gap. Fix: learn the rule, then drill that topic.
  • Knew it, rushed — a pacing problem. Fix: slow down on the early, easy questions where you are losing free points.
  • Misread it — an attention problem. Fix: underline what the question actually asks before you solve.
